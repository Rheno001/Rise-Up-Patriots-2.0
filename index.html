ABOUT RISE UP PATRIOTS CONFERENCE

The Rise Up, Patriots Conference 3.0 is a flagship programme of Unveiling and Rebranding Nigeria Initiative (URNI), themed "When Patriots Rise, Nations Transform."

As part of URNI's commitment to promoting patriotism, national rebranding, and active citizenship, this annual conference convenes policymakers, thought leaders, creatives for meaningful dialogue on patriotism, governance, innovation, and national development.

The conference serves as a platform for inspiring national pride, promoting positive narratives about Nigeria, and encouraging active citizenship. Through engaging discussions, networking opportunities, and practical learning experiences, participants are empowered to contribute to national development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the URNI Rise Up Patriot Conference 3.0 below.

The URNI Rise Up Patriots Conference 3.0 is a comprehensive gathering focused on patriotic values, community building, and national discourse. This conference brings together thought leaders, activists, and citizens to discuss important issues facing our nation and explore solutions for positive change.

The conference is open to all citizens who are passionate about patriotic values and community engagement. We welcome individuals from all backgrounds, professions, and walks of life who are interested in constructive dialogue and positive action for our country.

Registration can be completed online through our official website. Simply visit the registration page, fill out the required information, and submit your application.

Attendance at the Rise Up Patriots Conference 3.0 is completely free of charge. We welcome all interested participants to register and be part of this impactful event.

Yes, there will be a virtual attendance option. Once you complete your registration, a secure streaming link will be sent to you prior to the event.
